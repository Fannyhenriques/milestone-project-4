About Repose Spa and Wellness

Escape the stresses of everyday life and unwind at Repose Spa & Wellness, a quiet oasis in the heart of busy Stockholm. Our goal is to help you slow down and disconnect from technology, creating space for reflection and stimulation of the senses. Here you will find mindful activities, relaxing treatments, peaceful wellness areas and a spacious 200-square-metre gym.

Spa Access

Our reception is located by the main entrance, where you can book treatments and yoga sessions or make a reservation in our dining area if you have not already done so in advance. Day Spa access includes four hours of access to all spa, wellness and fitness areas, complimentary use of a bathrobe, towels and slippers, as well as access to our spa products, water, tea and fruit buffet. If you wish to stay longer or combine your spa visit with lunch, dinner or a treatment, take a look at our available packages.

Our Facilities

Discover spaces designed for rest, recovery and wellbeing. Our spa facilities include saunas, pools and dedicated relaxation areas.

  • 5 treatment rooms
  • A large indoor pool
  • Nordic sauna
  • Steam room
  • Jacuzzi
  • Relaxation room
  • Red light therapy

Fitness

Spa guests and members also have access to our fitness facilities. Our newly renovated 200-square-metre gym includes a dedicated yoga studio, where spa guests can book complimentary yoga classes during their visit. Members can also book yoga classes independently throughout their membership.
