Body Treatments

Body Scrub

A full-body invigorating salt scrub designed to refresh the skin and stimulate circulation. The treatment gently removes dull surface cells, leaving your skin feeling incredibly soft, smooth and radiant.

Mineral Body Wrap

A warming full-body wrap designed to soothe tired, stiff muscles and support recovery after physical activity. While the wrap takes effect, enjoy a relaxing face and scalp massage. The treatment is completed with a mineral-rich body cream blended with aromatic oils, leaving your skin soft and your body feeling restored.

60 minutes

Facials

Repose Signature Facial

A personalised facial experience beginning with a professional skin analysis, followed by gentle exfoliation, deep cleansing and a circulation-boosting facial massage. A nourishing mask is then applied to replenish and care for the skin. The experience is completed with a relaxing arm and hand massage. Suitable for all skin types.

60 minutes

Glow Facial

A deeply hydrating facial designed to restore moisture and natural radiance. Ideal for dull, tired or dehydrated skin, this treatment helps revive the complexion and enhance its natural glow. The treatment is tailored to your individual skin needs and combines gentle exfoliation, replenishing products and targeted care to leave the complexion feeling soft, smooth and refreshed.

45 minutes
